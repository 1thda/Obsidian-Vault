## Achieving and sustaining a Competitive Advantage - Locally and Globally 

**Competitive Advantage**: Edge that enables a company to outperform its average competitor in ways that matter to its customers.

## Competitive Advantage Tools 
- Porter's Competitive Forces Model: Five important forces that determine competitive power in a business situation and influence a company's position within a given industry. 

1) Threat of New Entrants 
	- Strength of barriers to entry and how easy is it to enter an industry. 
2) Supplier Power: Bargaining power of suppliers is high when a brand is powerful 
3) Buyer Power: The bargaining power of customers, buyers, and distribution channels is an opposite force to the bargaining power of suppliers 
4) Threat of substitute products or services 
5) Competitive Rivalry: Fierce competition leads to extensive advertising and promotions.
