## ISYS90026 - Concepts In Information Systems 

## Why Information Systems? 
- Systems as a combination of technologies (in a broader sense)
	  - Peoples + Processes + Information Systems 
- Assume that technology works -> How we can make the organization work better? 

Ensure that systems are **genuinely meaningful and valuable to users and solve real business problems** - Systems work in combination to ensure that everything works together. 

## Key Goals 
- Organizational efficiency and effectiveness 
- Data-drive decision making 
- Digital transformation 

**Case Studies** form the basis for a lot of the subject
- Business goals evolving? 
- How will new technologies integrate with existing systems? 
	- Will they complement the current infrastructure or require replacement? 
- Human impact -> how will employees respond to the changes 


### Skills that need to be developed  
- Analytical and interpretive skills through real-world business case analysis 
- Ability to evaluate business cases and contexts from multiple perspectives 
- Teamwork + Communication skills through participation and group case study analysis. 

## Strategic Mindset 
- **Strategy**: High-level plan or a set of decisions designed to achieve long-term goals or gain a competitive advantage 

Growth, Process, Problem-Solving, Development Goals... 

When proposing a new system, they should be understanding the goals 

- Strategy, planning exactly how to apply and integrate **information systems.**
	- Technology can help to maintain current strategies in the pre-existing mindsets 
		- develop into new markets

## System Thinking 
- Big picture as opposed to focusing on one thing. 
- Seeing the whole picture and recognizing that changes in one part of the system can have ripple effects throughout the entire system. 
![[Pasted image 20260802193657.png]]
- when you change one, you're pulling that edge and the other edges will also move. 

## Social Informatics 
- Technology is not a silver bullet 
- - Socio-technical design is key. 
- Understanding social dynamics is essential for creating technology that is truly useful and beneficial for people. 

Persuade people to buy into your system.
